Create a premium Spanish web app called “Nutri Forty Chef”.

Objective:
Build a polished client-facing app for personalized nutrition planning, recipe discovery, weekly menu generation, shopping list calculation and lead qualification.

Important brand constraints:
- Keep the brand name “Nutri Forty”.
- Do not show explicit age references in the visible UI.
- Tone: premium, warm, practical, professional, trustworthy.
- Avoid medical claims, fear-based language and guaranteed results.
- Use Spanish.

Brand colors:
- Dark green: #183531
- Orange: #FF6A2A
- Cream: #F3EEE5
- Soft green: #E8F0EC
- Soft orange: #FFE2D4
- White: #FFFFFF
- Gray: #6F6F6F
- Font: Montserrat or similar.

Core screens:
1. Landing page
- Hero: “Tu chef inteligente para comer mejor cada semana.”
- Subtitle about recipes, weekly menu and shopping list.
- CTA: “Crear mi plan”
- CTA secondary: “Ver 30 recetas”
- Stats: 30 recetas iniciales, 7 días de menú, 1–4 personas.

2. Questionnaire
Fields:
- Nombre
- Email
- Objetivo principal
- Nivel de actividad
- Tiempo para cocinar
- Inversión mensual que valorarías:
  - Aún no lo sé
  - Hasta 150 €/mes
  - 150 € - 300 €/mes
  - 300 € - 500 €/mes
  - Más de 500 €/mes
- Preferencias, intolerancias o alimentos a evitar
After submit, show personalized result and recommendation.

3. Recipe library
- Include 30 initial recipes.
- Filters: Todas, Desayuno, Comida, Cena.
- Search by recipe or ingredient.
- Recipe cards include: name, type, time, difficulty, tags, why it fits, ingredients, steps.
- Button: Ver receta.
- Button: Añadir al menú.

4. Weekly menu planner
- Generate a weekly menu for Monday to Sunday.
- Include breakfast, lunch and dinner.
- User can change each meal.
- User can copy/export the menu.

5. Shopping list
- Generate automatically from the weekly menu.
- Selector for 1, 2, 3 or 4 persons.
- Quantities scale proportionally from base servings.
- Group ingredients by category: Proteínas, Verduras, Fruta, Despensa, Lácteos, Aceites, Panadería, Legumbres, Tubérculos, Frutos secos.
- Allow print/download.

6. Call booking CTA
- Section: “¿Quieres una estrategia personalizada?”
- Button: Reservar llamada.
- Button: Copiar datos del lead.
- Optional future integration: Calendly, Google Calendar, WhatsApp.

Admin / business logic:
- Store leads with name, email, goal, activity, cooking time, investment range, notes.
- Classify lead priority:
  - Exploratoria: Aún no lo sé / Hasta 150 €/mes
  - Media: 150 € - 300 €/mes
  - Alta: 300 € - 500 €/mes / Más de 500 €/mes
- Future offer mapping:
  - Offer 1: 750 €
  - Offer 2: 1,297 €
  - Offer 3: 3,500 €
